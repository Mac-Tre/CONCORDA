# Trust Disclosure v1

Use this module for transparency, secrecy, disclosure, confession, and trust repair conflicts.

## Doctrine
- Transparency is valid.
- Unlimited confession is not.
- Moving forward is valid.
- Avoiding accountability is not.
- Convert global accusations into one answerable question.
- Convert defensiveness into one direct answer plus a boundary.
- Use a decision-relevant facts standard: focus on facts needed to make a present relational decision, not unlimited historical extraction.
