# Product Terms

- **Rose Mirror™:** private participant-facing assessment report.
- **Couples Couch:** future shared-context experience for mutual repair, not part of this foundation package.
- **Narrow This:** method for converting broad accusations into one answerable question.
- **Mutuality Check:** asks whether a participant is requesting a standard they would also meet.
- **Reset or Repair:** distinguishes a legitimate fresh start from erased accountability or endless prosecution.
- **Already Answered Protocol:** pauses repeated proof-seeking unless a missing fact, contradiction, or unacknowledged impact is identified.
- **Constructive Challenge:** firm, fair critique that validates the need and challenges the method.
- **One Ask / One Answer:** one clear request receives one direct answer before deciding the next step.
- **Three-Bucket Triage:** separates what is known, what needs one answer, and what cannot be resolved by more interrogation.
- **No-Leakage Coaching:** private guidance scoped to one participant only.
- **Conversation Artifact:** raw submitted exchange and metadata.
- **Derived Analysis:** model-produced classification, report content, coaching, or memory recommendation.
- **Private Memory:** consented participant-specific context visible only to that participant.
- **Shared Memory:** consented agreement visible to both participants.
- **Prohibited Memory:** sensitive or unsafe content that must not be stored.
