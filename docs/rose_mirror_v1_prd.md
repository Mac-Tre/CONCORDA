# Rose Mirror™ v1 Product Requirements

## Product purpose
Rose Mirror™ helps one participant privately examine a difficult relationship exchange and convert escalation into clear repair actions.

## User problem
People in conflict often need feedback that is direct enough to change behavior but careful enough not to diagnose, shame, or decide hidden facts.

## Target user
A participant in a difficult relationship conversation who wants a private assessment of their own communication pattern and a bounded next step.

## MVP promise
Rose Mirror™ will identify observable conflict patterns, surface the participant's strongest point and growth edge, provide a constructive challenge, and produce one next best message without leaking private coaching to anyone else.

## Core user flow
1. Paste/upload conversation.
2. Label participants.
3. Choose private assessment.
4. Safety and privacy notice.
5. Classify conflict.
6. Generate Rose Mirror assessment.
7. Show constructive challenge.
8. Provide next best message.
9. Offer daily practice.
10. Request memory consent.
11. Export PDF.

## Core features
- Rose Mirror participant report.
- Constructive Challenge.
- One Ask / One Answer.
- Three-Bucket Triage.
- Mutuality Check.
- Already Answered Protocol.
- No-Leakage Coaching.

## Non-goals
- Therapy diagnosis.
- Deciding who is right.
- Partner surveillance.
- Coercive confession.
- Unlimited interrogation.
- Automatic memory storage.
